[h]Lists[/h]

Lists are fun, right? Bullets. Numbers. Letters. Pick your poison. To start off, you use a list tag and then declare what type -- list=1 for numbered lists, or list=a for lettered list. It defaults to just bullets if you don't put anything. Surround your item with * tags.

[code]
[list][*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]

[list=a][*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]

[list=1][*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]

[/code]

gets

[list=*]
[*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]
[list=a]
[*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]
[list=1]
[*]item[/*]
[*]item[/*]
[/list]

Sorry, nested lists don't work here.
