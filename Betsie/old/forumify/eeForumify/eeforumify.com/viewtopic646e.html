Re: Tips on creating a consistent and dedicated crew.

Sorry about the bump, but I want to give some tips about getting crew members, and managing your crew, to make a  really good crew.


1. Leave it as open invitation at the start.

This may sound strange, but if you want to get popular, you first need to get lots of members, no matter how good. This is so that you can get a few levels out, however good, get a few thousand plays, and start to get more well known.

2. Choose the better, more active players.

If you do this, and get rid of noobs, you will get a much wider range of abilities playing your higher-quality levels. Improve older levels slowly, and start to find yourself getting better yourself.

3. Find out more about your crew.

Ask them how they like to build levels, whether they put codes at the end normally etc. This helps so that you know they are happy with what they are doing. Find out who is best at what, for example, who is good at art, and who is good at minis. Ask them if they feel comfortable in the crew, or if there is anything that could be improved.

4. Check levels before you let them in.

If anyone ever wants to join the crew, check their ability by checking there worlds. If they are not very good, don't be afraid to say no to them. You want to keep your crew under control, so you only have good players in there.

5. Make pro levels, get pros in crew.

Try hard to make really good levels and get a really good team, and you never know, you may get someone reasonable famous on EE to ask to join. This could be a real boost to your team, because people then know that your team is really good and dedicated.

6  Enjoy the game and have fun!

This is important because if you don't enjoy the game, you aren't going to get anywhere.


And now, a few tips

Never Give Up

Never give up, making levels and getting a good crew is a long and tiring process. If you don't have lots of time on your hands, don't even bother making or joining a crew. If this is the case, just enjoy playing the levels that other people have made, and possibly create a world of your own every so often. But the main thing is that you are willing to invest time to make the most of things.

Don't Always Worry About It, Take Breaks

If all you do is worry about looking after the crew, take a break. If you work at it whenever things need doing, as soon as they need doing, you will rush it without knowing, because you want to get it done. Take a break from making sure everything is correct, and play some other games, read a chapter of a book, and then get back to work, and don't stress about it.



Thanks For Reading, And Good Luck Getting A Nice Crew!

Disclaimer: Sorry if some of this is in the Main Post, but I thought I would share how I would create a successful crew.
