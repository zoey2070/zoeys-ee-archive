Revamped Warning System

We have gone ahead and revamped our warning system - any warnings gained after this point will be recorded using the new system.  This allows us to know what each warning is for AND in the order they were acquired.  This will hopefully reduce confusion regarding warnings.

As a bonus, anyone who posts here may ask for a current warning count (which will be posted on this topic), and will have one warning SUBTRACTED from your current count if you already have more than 1 warning,.  Don't miss out on this opportunity to get a warning removed!

There will be no further warning removals for the time being.  You may still ask for a warning count, however.
