Coin Effects
Decrease Clock: Some blocks are taken out from the timer. Does not spawn any coins from orange blocks being taken out, though.
Increase Clock: Some blocks are added back to the timer. Does not place any orange blocks.
Freeze Clock: Temporarily stops the clock, and stops editing anywhere in the game zone (except to place backgrounds).
Star Gun (rare): Press space to fire stars! Hold a direction while pressing space to fire it in a set direction. If you don't hold a direction, the stars will go in the last direction you pressed. If somebody (besides you) gets hit by a star, a 5x5 area of paint around them will be erased!
Fill in the Blank: Replaces all blank blocks with your color. Can be extremely helpful early in the match, and not so helpful later.
Paint Gun (a tiny bit less rare than the Star Gun): Fires 1x1 paint! Works like the Star Gun. Bullets will appear as Normal BG instead of Dark BG (or the other way around)
Alternate for Blue Coins: 15% of the time, when someone picks up a coin, the bot will announce that it's a Blue Coin! Then, a negative coin effect happens on the user who picked it up. To make up for this, though, good coins will then spawn closer to you and Blue Coins will spawn farther away from you.
