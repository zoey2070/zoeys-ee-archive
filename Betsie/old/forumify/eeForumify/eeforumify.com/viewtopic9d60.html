New feature. - Warnings

Admins / mods should now give more comprehensive warnings and the forum will allow you to view your warnings.

Mods: look for Warn Report at the bottom of a post. It's in grey.
 
Warning types and warning levels can then be viewed by forum users by going to warnings.php in your profile.

Also warnings now expire after a set period of time to be defined by the moderator depending on the severity of the infraction.

EDIT: I have added warning types to make it easier to issue warnings. There is also a custom warning section allowing moderators to issue custom warnings if an offence is not listed.
