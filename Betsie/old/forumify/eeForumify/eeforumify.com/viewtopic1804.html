Re: Everybody Edits Easter 2011: The winners!

In any online community game, there are items that are rare and exclusive, meaning not everyone will be able to collect everything. I think the problem here is that up until this point, there's been really no exclusive content on EE save for the Christmas and New Year's packs, so players have become accustomed to being able to buy absolutely everything.

The prizes for the contest are possibly the first content that will be truly exclusive, and as a result many players are protesting since this hasn't happened before on EE. IMO, this means that once the precedent is set for exclusive smilies being released via special promotions, these sorts of complaints will die down.

So given everything I've posted, it's clear the smilies should be sold for gems at a later date if the mods and Chris do not wish to make any content on EE exclusive. However, if the mods and Chris do want to have rare and exclusive items, then it's critical to not release these smilies so everyone can adjust to the fact that collecting absolutely everything is not possible in EE just like it's not possible in practically every other online game.

Deciding not to have any exclusive content will please many players, but will also ensure that contests and other promotions lack a truly motivating prize. Deciding to have exclusive content will irritate many players in the short run, but in the long run people will come to accept it and contests can continue to have a real motivator.

BTW, my opinion has been changed due to the recent posts on this topic and now I am leaning towards not releasing the smilies, so everyone can learn to accept exclusivity of some items in EE so we can move on from this.
