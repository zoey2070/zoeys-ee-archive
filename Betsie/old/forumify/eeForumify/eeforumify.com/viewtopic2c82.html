Resistance

Foreword

We can change our opinions over the time. And that's totally okay. But when do we change them?
    The resistance focuses on bringing valuable feedback about problems at Everybody Edits or any other games they are active on. This group is about finding friends, changing the place we waste our time in, and making it a bit more worth being there. everybody can join. but the connecting to the core of the resistance requires a application. If you are part of the core you are allowed to contribute to our guidelines and get the ability to connect even deeper with us. Everyone has the same rights at the core. no leader.

FAQ
Q: can i be the leader? - A: i recommend reading the foreword again.
Q: if i am in the core, how can i join the private meetings? - A: send me a message with your skype nick.

+ the all mighty guardians
+ other problems
