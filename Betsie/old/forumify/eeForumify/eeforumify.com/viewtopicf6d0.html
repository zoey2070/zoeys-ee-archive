The thing about memes is that they are effectively fill-in-the-blank comedy. You have the exact same joke repeated, but with the content changed. The repetition simultaneously removes the creativity and adds familiarity, making the joke easily accessible to the lowest common denominator.

For the most part, this results in a few genuinely funny jokes, but ultimately derives into shallow, overused garbage. (See: doge, gr8 m8, etc.)

Personally, I'm not a huge fan.
