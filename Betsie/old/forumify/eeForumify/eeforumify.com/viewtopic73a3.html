It could be as a browser addon, in which something appears next to the level on the everybodyedits.com website.

skullz16 wrote:

How would you categorise the levels to find out what kind of levels a player likes?

Right now, we're not sure exactly how it'll recommend levels, maybe finding players with similar interests. The crawler records and analyzes all events (except chat), and knows which worlds you've been to, for how long, how much you interacted with it, how many woots you gave it, how many times you returned, whether you were kicked, how far you travelled, interactions with other players, how quickly you completed the level compared to others, how long you idled in the level, title of the level (to an extent), your magic class, whether you were god mode at any time*, your profile (which items you bought and when*), how often the level was changed/modified, how many users played the level, how many people were in the room when you were there, and much more.

Edit: the problem is, we might need much more data than we have now, possibly years and years worth of events, in order to get an accurate prediction. However, we'll see what we can do.
