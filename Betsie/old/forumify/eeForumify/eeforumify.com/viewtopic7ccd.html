Official IRC Channel

Hi forums!

I'm pleased to announce we now have an official IRC channel. This is just a test period, we will see how it does.

Since I know that IRC can be confusing for some, I've taken the liberty of integrating the web client into the forums.

Try the channel out! THE IRC CHANNEL IS NO LONGER OFFICIALLY ACTIVE AT THIS TIME, USE IT AT YOUR OWN RISK

You ought to register your nick so it can't be stolen by someone else.

Our rank system:

Me = ~ (Owner)
Forum admins = & (Protected ops)
Forum mods = @ (Ops)
Chat mods = % (Halfops)
Chat regulars = + (Voice)

I also have a bot in there, he's got Ops so if things get out of control, he can take care of the channel. (use !help to find out his command list, and he automatically translates text to English)

WARNING: Being chat, it has the potential to get inappropriate. DO NOT JOIN THE CHAT IF YOU ARE EASILY OFFENDED.

For those of you with actual IRC clients, join irc.rizon.net:6667 on #eeforum


Enjoy, and remember, this is just a test period. We can deal with abusive users much better in IRC than in TinyChat.
