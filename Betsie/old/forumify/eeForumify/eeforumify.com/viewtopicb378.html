Okay, *tries to remember the past year and a half and everything that happened (a lot of stuff)*

2012(since Tako's post above) EE updates: Magic coins, magic class and woots got added into the game. Potions had a certain level you needed to be to buy them. Jungle package with headhunter smiley came got added. Jungle backgrounds and vines added. Builder's Club added, Beta no longer available for purchase. Checkpoints, spikes and Christmas 2012 update.

2013 EE updates: MRVOID joins, and we get the winter smiley. Lava blocks, fire hazard and fire potion added. Fire demon and curse potion added. Smileys need a certain class to buy them. Bishop smiley, potion of protection, new promo video and /teleport added. Zombie potion, terms and conditions, /potionson and /potionsoff added. Swamp and respawn potion added and /respawn removed. Bruce smiley, /visible, /getpos and /help added. Zombie doors and gates added. Open worlds got limited to only basic blocks, metal blocks, gravity and crown in order to bring back EE how it used to be. Sci-fi 2013, invisible portals announced and featured worlds added. Unit smiley and invisible portals added (and the people who submitted worlds get some for free). Potion of levitation added. Ancient Greek blocks, columns and Sparta smiley added. Helen of Sparta, potion of flaunt, signs and /reportabuse added. Farm package added. Moo moo smiley, scarecrow smiley and home worlds added. Chat became free so every registered account gets chat automatically. /mute and /unmute added. Potion of solitude added and smiley creating competition started. Competition ends. Kung Fu Master by Tiken, Fox by Cola1 and Night Vision by Toop win the competition and the dark wizard smiley is added. Summer girl smiley added, MrShoe 'goes on holiday' and Mrvoid removes magic decay, 19th June 2013.

THEN, the game isn't updated until 30th April 2014. Someone used a bot to go to every world and crash it. Atilla tries to create things to prevent it, but the crashing continues. Atilla (and possibly some other people, not sure) contacts Brian about the problem and finally devs come to try and fix the problem.
