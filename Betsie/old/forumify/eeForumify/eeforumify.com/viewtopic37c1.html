Re: The top 10 levels are up! Vote for your favourite on the blog now!

The judges that picked the ten rooms appear to like levels where you don't always know where to go next. Personally, I prefer to know where to go next, mostly because I usually try to collect all coins.

3. Team Xile; The 2011 Year Old Tree: Average minimap, basic/easy route, not always clear where to go next.
8. EMBUR; Reversible New Year: Impressive minimap, basic/easy route (except for some blue coins).
12. Lx Crew; New year New concept: Average minimap, no fun/insane route (I stopped playing).
22. MG Crew; MG Crew New Years: Nice minimap, the "easy" route is easy, the "hard" route is easy until the crown (the section after it is annoying, and why continue when you have the crown).
23. EECHAT; Visions of Red: Short and very easy route, average minimap and the drawings are not really connected to each other.
31. Fireworks Galaxy; New Year Galaxy: Most of the minimap is not really new year related and average, plus the drawings aren't really connected to each other, the 'route' is not interesting and too easy (not even counting getting the crown in three seconds).
32. Who the F is Starblinky; The Clock Strikes 12: My personal favorite and the room I voted for. Its minimap is good, the route is challenging (but a bit too difficult in the Western tree section).
33. BA Team; BA Team RIP 2010: I'm not impressed by the minimap and the route is too easy.
88. The Adams; 2011 calender: Unimpressive minimap, too many coins to waste time getting them all, route basically just a bunch of minigames with too many repetitive ninja-jumps.
100. Team Blabla; YOUR NEW YEAR: Average minimap, "work out" is difficult and not that enjoyable, both "winner party" and "get smarter" are original and fun to play, but over in no time.
