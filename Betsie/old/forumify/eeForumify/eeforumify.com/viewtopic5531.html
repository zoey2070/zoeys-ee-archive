PaintMEE


Level | Teams | Highscores | Trello board

Description

PaintMEE is a bot asisted level in which you have to paint game area with color of your team. When the game starts, you are teleported inisde of the level and by simply moving around it you're able to paint background with color corresponding to your team. From time to time inside of level appears coin, which when collected executes random powerup that can help your team to win the game.

Credits

Thanks to terrorcokkie, lictor666, stagecrew, mylo, dclevels and everyone else who came to the world and helped me to test the bot while I was working on it. I wouldn't manage to finish it without you.


Map submission

Name: (Name of your map - What you want the PaintMEE to display when selecting your level)
Creators: (People who made the map)
Level link: (Link to the level)

Guidelines for the submissions:
    1. All map submissions must be made in a medium (50x50) world.
        * This is to make it easy for my bot to save the maps.

    2. There can't be any gravity/empty blocks separated from rest.
        * Note that I might accepts levels where these areas are separate and have exact count of the gravity blocks, but chances for that are small.

    3. You can't use any action blocks that are limited (signs, portals, coin gates).
        * Spawn points are allowed.

    4. PainMEE levels should also have many shortcuts, no minigames and be fun to play.


Ideas submission

If you have any great ideas for PaintMEE you can share them here. You should give them a good description to make it easy for me to implement.

Please note that I can reject or edit any of the provided submissions.

I have some suggestions for this bot:

Coin Effects
Platform: Some blue sci-fi one-way platforms appear near the user who picked up the coin. The length varies.
Smaller Paint: Basically the opposite of Bigger Paint.
Higher Jump: Places two up arrows wherever the user jumps.
Paint Shuffle: (rare) Replaces the most common paint on the board with the most uncommon paint on the board, the second most common paint on the board with the second most uncommon paint on the board, etc. etc.
Coin Blurb: (very rare) Instead of rewarding the user with a powerup, it says a silly line such as "Money count increased by one coin!" or "You turn around the coin and find the word 'gullible' written on it!" and doesn't have any effect.

Items
Blue Coins: They're like yellow coins, but only the negative effects happen, and they happen on the user that picked it up!

Game Modes
Scrubber: It's the exact opposite of the normal mode! There is paint (pastel) that is pre-placed in the level that the players have to scrub off. Scrubbing off red paint is worth one point, yellow paint is two and is slightly rarer than red paint, green paint is three points, and is rarer than yellow paint, and only two blocks of cyan paint appear each round, and are worth ten points! After the paint is scrubbed off, the blocks are replaced by the dark bg blocks of the scrubber's team. After 90% of the paint runs out, the whole playing area is re-painted.
