All the Shortcuts, Hotkeys & Commands Ingame!

Thanks to noelzkie for the original topic

General
M - Minimap.
U - Uses selected potion.
Esc - Close the current menu that is popped up.

Movement
Up or W - move up
Down or S - move down
Left or A - move left
Right or D - move right

Building
1,2...0 - The numbers correspond with the numbers in the quickbar. Click here to see how
Ctrl or Shift -  Select the first brick in the quickbar.
G - Godmode.
Tab - See all the blocks.

Chat
Shift - See the chat bubbles and the names while moving.
T or Enter - Pop up the chatbar.
Tab - autocomplete a username.

Player management
/respawnall
/giveedit <name>
/removeedit <name>
/kick <name> [reason]
/kill <name>
/killemall
/teleport <x> <y> [name]
/reportabuse <name> <reason>
/kickguests
/mute <name>
/unmute <name>

World management
/loadlevel
/reset
/potionson
/potionsoff
/visible <true|false>
/getpos [name]
/listportals


Key:
<required parameter>
<this|or this>
[optional parameter]
You need to own the level to use this.

I probably forgot some, tell me here
