Amazing Noobs (World List)

Hello! Welcome to my new world list! But, this isn't like the others. These worlds are made by adequate noobies with little or no skill. Why make this world list if no one cares about noobs, you ask? Well, first of all, it's to promote some lesser-known players into EE. I bet you never heard of Retlegos or Domakesaythink, right? Well, this is what this list is for. These worlds are usually not appealing and full of terrible minigames and bland art, but just think of this: were you able to make this world when you first stepped into EE? Yes you would because you are a sexy beast. No, just kidding.

Because these worlds aren't top-notch EX Crew levels, I will be criticizing them using constructive criticism. In other words, I will only be saying good.
The list starts right now:

***WARNING! NOOB WORLDS APPROACHING!***

THESE ARE NOT WORLDS MADE BY NSFW OR TOOP/BADOOSH. THESE ARE 100% HAND-CRAFTED BY THE EE NOOB POPULATION. PLAY AT YOUR OWN RISK!

12-28-2014
Donkey Kong
by Blazer124

Even though the minis were quite unprofessional, the remake of a Donkey Kong level in EE was quite common, but this one doesn't compare to the rest. The (almost) perfect art really does prove it was made by an experienced builder, but nope.

http://everybodyedits.com/games/PWS5nputC0bkI

12-28-2014
Just Jump
by superxsuper

Yes, he may be widely known, but his worlds are something you wouldn't find yourself playing with friends. His worlds are guaranteed to be fun for all different kinds of EE players, from the general population to those who completed Forgotten Veil....twice. Anyway, it was quite fun to play. It did take a while, even with the simple platforming jumps.

http://everybodyedits.com/games/PWFSUHECGIbUI

12-27-2014
Difficulty: Hard
by Retlegos

Despite the misleading name, this level really brings me back to the old days. I mean, not all blocks were nostalgic, but the maze-like structures and Mario Bros-oriented positions really makes you want to explore and collect all of the 816 coins. Even though there were many broken minis and exploits, it was worth spending 45 minutes of your life with. Trust me.

http://everybodyedits.com/games/PWnCixcShtbUI
