A quick little Portal tutorial.

This tutorial will cover the most basic functions of the new portal system and will not be describing any elaborate uses for it. That is entirely up to the player to discover.

A very basic room will be used to demonstrate the uses of portals!

Connecting the portals

1. The first thing you'll notice when you click on the portal icon is that these two counters will show up.

-    The top one is asking for the ID of the current portal you're going to place. For starters, I'll pick the number 1.
-    The other one right below is asking for what portal you will end up in when going through it. Naturally, we'll pick 2 for this one.

2. There we go! We have now placed the very first portal in the map. However, since there is no second portal to go to, it's fairly useless right now, don't you think?

3. That's why we are going to place another portal in the second room! This is where the importance of keeping track of portal ID numbers come in to play.

Portal '1' has the exit linked to '2', so going through the first portal will make you end up in the second room. Also, since portal '2' uses '1' as its exit ID, you will end up right back where you started if you go through portal '2'. Easy stuff.

Turning the portals direction

Now I will be telling you how to change the direction of the portals. As you might have noticed, there is no "Change direction" button anywhere in the information bubble, so you need to do it this way instead.

By clicking on the level, a portal will appear facing left by default. If you want to make the portal face upwards instead, click on the portal that you just spawned one more time. Click it again to make it face right and click it one more time to make it face downwards. The picture illustrates how many clicks are needed to make the portal face the desired direction.

Connecting multiple portals

Another fun thing about portals is that they don't have to be paired up 1 and 1, you can assign whatever ID's you like so you can easily for example make an entire cluster of portals linked to a single exit portal. The example above shows two '1' portals that are linked to a '2' portal that in turn links back to the '1' portals.

----

I hope this quick tutorial will help you guys out in understanding the basic mechanics of portals. Hopefully, you will all make some amazingly creative levels soon.
