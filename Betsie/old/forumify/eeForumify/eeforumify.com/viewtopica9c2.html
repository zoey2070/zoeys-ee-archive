Bowser´s Castle

Hello guys another time! I am making another world called Bowser´s Castle.

http://everybodyedits.com/games/PW7a91iTpxbkI

As you see, it is an rpg. This castle appears in the game Mario and Luigi Superstar Saga. I loved it, so I copied the maps, fights, bosses... At the start, you´ll find some signs where you see two enemies talking. If you have never played the game, I'll explain to you who are Fawful and Bowletta:

Bowletta: Cackletta, an evil witch, went to Mushroom Kingdom to take Peach´s voice to wake the Beanstar and get its power. Peach knew her evil plans, so she wore a Birdo like her. Mario, Luigi and Bowser went to Bean Bean Kingdom to save it from Cackletta. The Beanstar became mad when heard Birdo´s voice and escaped with Bowser in the air. Meanwhile, the Mario Bros. defeated Cackletta. Her minion, Fawful, vacuumed her soul by his Fawfulcopter and escaped to find a body and save Cackletta. He found Bowser unconcious after falling by the Beanstar and gave him the soul. Bowser became Bowletta and used his flying castle to take the Bean Bean Kingdom and then the Mushroom Kingdom.

Fawful: Is Bowletta´s minion, as I explained before.

Now you must reach the end of the castle and defeat Bowletta once for all. Can you do it?

This world is unfinished yet, but it will be finished. I'll try to finish it soon, but I don't know how much time will it take.

Don't ask me for edit because I will never give you edit. If you are stuck or you don't know how to continue, tell me it. The url of the world appears at the start of this comment.

Thanks. There are 4 Mario and Luigi games. Superstar Saga was the first, but it was my favourite one. I haven´t got that game, I only saw videos about it. I have Mario and Luigi: Partners in Time and Bowser´s Inside Story, and soon Dream Team.

By the way, there is a difference between SuperStar Saga Castle and Bowser´s Inside Story Castle. Superstar Saga´s Bowser´s Castle is a flying castle that Bowletta took from the Mushroom Kingdom. That mean the main castle doesn´t appear in that game. In Bowser´s Inside Story, we play in the Mushroom Kigdom, and we play the main Castle. Their maps are different, and also you can not fight the Koopalings in the second Castle.

+ Info: As you see in the world, there are lots of coin doors and gates. Near them, there are doors. Those coin doors and gates will be opened/closed if you beat the boss at the end of each room. How to go to each room? Use the doors. If you don't beat the boss, you can not pass. Also if you beat the boss, you can use an invisible portal to get teleported to behind the door. That lets you lose less time.

++ Info: Some players told me that avoiding enemies´ attacks was kinda hard. That´s why I put checkpoints near them. Also, don't ask me why are not 10 coins that let you pass the first part of the map. First, I want to finish the main map (the map that starts in castle´s entrance and finishes in Bowletta´s room). When it is finished, I'll start making the seven Koopalings´ rooms. Also, if when I finish the world, I have some space, I'll make the castle sprite. It looks cool!
