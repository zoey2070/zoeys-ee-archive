Recent Forum Changes

I'm sure all of you have seen the recent changes to the forum.  To sum them up, here is all we did:

- Moved the feedback category above the off-topic category.
- Merged 'forum discussion' into 'forum suggestions' and renamed it 'forum discussion'.
- Created a 'topic graveyard' and moved most locked topics in there.  (If you find a locked topic (announcements don't count) outside the graveyard, REPORT IT!)
- Create a support forum for questions about EE and the like.

We are also thinking about making a few more changes, and we'd like your opinion on them:

- Create an 'entertainment' forum to talk about anything regarding entertainment: TV, movies, and other art forms.
- Delete the introductions forum.

Post on what you think we should do and any feedback for these changes.  Thanks!
