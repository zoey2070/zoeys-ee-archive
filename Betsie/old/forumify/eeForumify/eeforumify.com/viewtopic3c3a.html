Re: Everybody Edits Halloween 2011: The winners!

I agree with most of the people in this topic that there should be a re-do. Surely more and better judges can be conjured up (No offence to RpgMaster2000, but I find it difficult and somewhat unfair for just one person to vote hundreds of levels alone).

Levels should not be based purely or mostly off art. Seeing how that's almost always the case and not everyone is pro at art (making it less fair for some, who have great minis)
Levels should be based EQUALLY of...
1. Art (Decent art? [Doesn't have to be pro])
2. Quality / Difficulty Level of Minigames (Not too hard, but not too easy... Playable for everyone)
3. Originality / Uniqueness of Minigames (Not using the same minigame over and over)
4. Fun (How fun was playing the level overall?)

That's how I would judge a level... This is just my opinion, but I strongly believe that Art currently takes precedence over all others (Which it should not, everything should be judged equally)
Also, favoritism should not be used. Don't judge a level as superior just because your in the crew of the people who made the level or like / know the person.

This is just very unfair to people who've worked hard on their levels, especially after making modifications to their levels due to a silly update. Which reminds me, NO updates should be made during a contest as it could boot people out who may have forgotten to save their levels and spent hours working on it. Which causes a lot of aggravation and stress to people. OR you could use an idea I've thought of for this... Make a warning dialog that appears on the screen that would say something like "The game will be updated in 5 minutes, make sure you save your level so you don't lose your work" and make it do some kind of ding sound (like a coin?) to grab attention incase the user is in another tab or window.

This is just my 2 cents though. I mean no offence to anyone.
