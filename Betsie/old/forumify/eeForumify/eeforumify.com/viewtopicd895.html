Petition to stop usage of harmful potions and hazards - January 29, 2015

Recently, there has been a petition, along with a survey, from Everybody Edits players that the zombie and curse potions, fire and spikes, and /kill commands be banned from ever being used, with over 500 votes supporting the petition. The creator of the petition, says "These things are doing nothing but bringing people to torture of having to die and be reborn again, and that's not fun—whoever designed these blocks, potions, and features should have thought more about the consequences of them."

A minor member of the staff team has responded saying that the staff team is considering taking action against this, though it is not confirmed if such will happen. "We might do something if we get some more votes," reports replied aforementioned staff member.

Supporters have responded by turning off curse and zombie potions, replacing spikes and fire with harmless portals, and not using /kill commands against players. 89% of supporters say that death should not be treated casually, but as something very serious. 36% say that death penalty should never by a world owner or Guardian.

What do you think about such usage? Feel free to share your opinions in the comments. We hope to keep you updated in any significant announcements.
