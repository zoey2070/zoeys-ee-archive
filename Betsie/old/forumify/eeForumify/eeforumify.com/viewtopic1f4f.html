The Adventure of Hinault

Lone lost on the Planet Nonsense
you find yourself in yet another, amazing adventure of danger and treasures.
Not only you travel through the vast regions of ancient,
cold and forgotten ruins of civlisations long lost,
but you descent into the depths of hell to return for your glorious victory over the monsters of Nonsense!
You are Hinault, the badger and you give a ♥♥♥♥ about danger, ragequit and edit.


This is an adventure map and you are supposed to go back and forth at some points, revisiting places you have been to already - to collect more stuff.
