And then there was one. [Everyody Edits Murder Story]

Alright, so I've been working on writing a murder story sort of like the one Brawler posted 2 or so years ago. This story is based off an old book/movie titled: "And then there were none." I don't want to spoil too much for those who haven't read the book or seen the movie, but basically 10 people are trapped on an island, and one by one, all of them will die, until only the murderer remains.


I will be releasing the story one chapter at a time, to allow you guys to discuss who you think the murderer is. Also, the order of the deaths and the murderer have been determined already. I will not base the deaths off of who you guys think it is.


Now let's get started


Chapter 1: The Introduction


          MrShoe decided it was time to host the first official gathering for EE players around the globe. He ran a contest to select ten lucky players to go on the trip, he couldn’t afford to take a lot. The goal of the contest was to be one of the first ten users to find a new magic coin, and receive the light wizard. The chances of finding this rare smiley, were one in a million. After several weeks, the contest had ended and these were the ten winners: Benje00, Boh, DCLevels, Failgirl101, Gkabyss, Glenn21, Honeybuckles44, Reidolol, Stagecrew, and Theditor.
   

          The trip began with a long boat ride to an abandoned island named Prolantis. MrShoe wanted a nice private island, to allow his plans to go smoothly without interruptions. “So, how is everyone enjoying the trip so far?” said MrShoe.“ I think it’s great, I love getting to actually meet everyone!” replied Honeybuckles44. “When we get to the island, can we go swimming to find lampreys?” questioned Gkabyss. “Lampreys, really? you’ll probably just go afk as soon as we get there,” said Theditor. “No Gka, when we get there we’ll need to journey deep into the heart of the island. According to my map, there should be a house around there,” answered MrShoe. “Once we get all set up and move in, you can go swimming.” Glenn21 then turned around and looked at the sky with horror on his face. “Do you guys see that massive dark cloud up there!?” “Oh no, hold on guys there might be a storm coming,” replied MrShoe nervously. “MIGHT BE A STORM COMING? THERE WILL BE A STORM COMING!” Screamed a shocked Failgirl101. “Okay Failgirl calm down, I’m sure MrShoe will get us there safely,” said Benje00 Calmly. Just then, a loud roar filled the air and a blinding flash struck dead in the center of the ship. The boat was ripped apart and the members of the crew were sent overboard, only one hundred feet from the island shore.


          As the water began tossing him around, a panicking Reidolol screamed ”Help! I don’t know how to swim!” Benje00, who was the only one nearby at the time, was able to help him get a bit closer to shore. Once he couldn’t bear the weight anymore, he turned to the left and saw Gkabyss, who was the best swimmer of the group. “Hey Gka, I’m struggling to keep both of us afloat, would you mind taking Reidolol for me?” “Sure,” responded Gka. Benje00 handed over Reidolol, and began hurrying to the shore. “I’m a pretty bad swimmer too, but I should be able to handle just myself.” Said Benje00. “Ha, you noob” Shouted DCLevels, about 30 feet in front of Benje00. “This is easy, I’ll be at the shore in no time” “Same here,” yelled Stagecrew, another ten feet in front of DClevels. After about fifteen minutes, Glenn was the first one to reach the island. Stagecrew made it shortly after, and then one by one, DClevels, Boh, Honey, Theditor, and Failgirl all safely reached land.


          After about ten more minutes had passed, Gkabyss and Reidolol finally emerged from the water together, closely followed by Benje00. All ten contest winners had safely made it to the island. They then decided to discuss the madness that just occurred. “I can’t believe this horrible luck we just had!” Said Failgirl, outraged. “Horrible luck? We won a contest with the odds heavily against us, one in one million!” Said Gkabyss sarcastically. “Oh won’t you just shut up!” Yelled Failgirl. “Oh won’t you just be nice for once,” Said Honeybuckles. After arguing about the recent experience for nearly an hour, they agreed that they needed to go and find a place to stay for the night. “A place to stay,” thought Boh “A house… Wait a second. WHERE IS MrShoe!?”






Chapter 2: The First Night


          Gasps filled the island. “Did anyone see him in the water after the boat was torn apart?” Asked Failgirl. “No” replied everyone in sync. “Do you guys think he might be… dead…” Said Stagecrew curiously. “I’m not sure, there is a small chance that could still be alive out in the ocean somewhere,” replied DCLevels. “The boat was ripped apart, but there are still some floating pieces of wood that he could use to keep afloat.” Theditor then spoke “I say forget him for now. We need to find a safe place to stay. There’s only about one hour of daylight remaining.” “MrShoe mentioned a house in the center of the island, but without his map I’m not sure if we’ll ever find it,” said Benje00. “Well, we’ll have to stay somewhere, house or not. Let’s get moving,” replied Theditor. The group of ten then journeyed further into the island and eventually became lost in the darkness.


          “Does anyone have a clue where we are right now?” Questioned Boh. “No idea, it feel like we travelling for ages,” said Reidolol. “Yeah. This island must be huge,” said Honeybuckles in agreement. “GUYS! Look over there!” shouted Glenn, pointing off to the right. “I see a cave, we could stop there for the night!” “Are you kidding me?! ” yelled Failgirl. “I’m not sleeping in that filthy cave! Who knows what we’ll find in there!” “It doesn’t seem like there’s anything living it,” said Gkabyss. “I agree with Glenn, we should camp out here for tonight.” “NO! I refuse to sleep here, and how do you know nothing is living in there? It might just be gone for now, but planning to come back!” screamed Failgirl. “Come on, it won’t be that bad. Hmm… What if someone kept watch over night,” Said Glenn21. “We could put one person in charge of alerting us, if anything dangerous shows up.” “What a great idea!” shouted Stagecrew. “I’ll take the first watch, I’m not really tired right now.” They then moved into the cave and set up a place to sleep. All of them had their own sleeping area, except Stagecrew, who remained just outside the cave entrance. “When I get tired, I’ll wake one of you guys up to take over, alright?” “Sounds good,” Replied everyone in agreement.


          The silence of the night put everyone into a deep sleep. All that could be heard were the low soft snores of the nine tired EE players. Hour after hour went by. The world began to fade darker and darker as the night went on. Midnight struck with a loud scream of terror. Several shocked players jumped onto their feet and looked around. It was nearly impossible for them to see anything, for the trees had blocked out most of the moonlight. All was pitch black. A second scream then exploded into the darkness. “AHHHHHHH!!!!!” screamed Boh horrified. “It’s Stagecrew! His head!  It looks like his skull has been smashed in!” And sure enough, Stagecrew’s skull had been shattered. As blood poured out of his destroyed head, DClevels looked off to the side. Then he saw it. laying about 2 feet from Stagecrew was a bloody cracked rock, about the size of a bowling ball.

~ Ten EE Players resting in a line; One suffered a migraine, and then there were nine.






Chapter 3: From Bad to Worse


          As the night continued, nobody else slept for fear that something might happen to them. They all huddled together in a corner at the back of the dark-stained cave. The night felt as if it lasted forever. Eventually the sun began to ascend off in the distance, and the now-group-of-nine finally exited the cave. “Hmm, this is very strange,” Said Gkabyss. “what’s strange?” questioned DCLevels. “Well, I find it odd that he died like this, I mean, It’s as if he was murdered by someone,” answered Gka. “I really don’t see why that’s strange, he was obviously murdered, the rock isn’t going to fall from the sky,” Said DClevels. “This island is supposed to be abandoned, nobody else is here. Plus with that storm still going on, I’m sure nobody new will arrive for a long time,” said Gka. Glenn then jumped into the conversation, “Well, what if there are other people on the island and MrShoe just didn’t know about it?” “That’s another thing, the ground out here is soaked from all the rain. There would be footprints if someone had approached this cave last night, but I don’t see any,” replied Gkabyss. “Very interesting. I never thought of that,” Said Glenn. “Guys, can we stop talking about the death for five minutes. Benje00 and Theditor seem really upset over this,” came Honeybuckles’ voice out of nowhere.


          Everyone gathered around as Benje00 and Theditor were sitting next to the dead body of a great friend. Theditor was able to hold back his tears and keep calm. Benje00 however, took the death far worse. He cried over the death of one of his best friends, and all the other players helped to comfort him. It took an hour for Benje00 to finally pull himself together. “Alright… Let’s get going I guess… We really need to find the house before nightfall this time. We can’t let this happen to anyone else.” “Agreed,” said Reidolol. They began their journey once again, however this time, it only lasted about five minutes. “THERE IT IS!” Shouted Failgirl full of excitement. “Finally! Now let’s get in there before anything terrible happens to us.”


          The house was two stories tall and contained five bedrooms, two bathrooms, a living room, a kitchen, a dining room, and a basement. They decided the safest way to do things, was to pair up three to a room and keep the last two bedrooms empty. The pairings were as follows: Failgirl, Honey, and Boh in the first room, Gkabyss, DCLevels, and Reidolol in the second, and finally Glenn, Benje00, and Theditor in the third. After moving in, and getting settled as best as they could, everyone went to the kitchen to see if there was any food. “Ugh, of course the refrigerator is practically empty” Said an upset Failgirl. “We could go down by the water and try to catch some fish,” said Glenn thoughtfully. “No! Don’t hurt the fish!” Shouted Gkabyss. “Look, do you want them to die, or do you want YOU to die” Said Glenn with a strange look on his face. “What do you mean?” Asked Gka. “Well…If we don’t kill them, you’ll probably die of starvation,” Answered Glenn. “Ugh, just go find food, we’ll set up to cook whatever you guys bring back” Said Boh.


          The six guys left the house with a few kitchen knives and made their first attempt to hunt. Within a matter of minutes, DClevels had his first kill, a deer. “Wow DC, I didn’t know you were so good at throwing knives, I wonder how good you are at throwing rocks,” Said Theditor. “What are you trying to say? That I killed Stagecrew!?” Said DClevels, now starting to rage. “Well, one of us had to have killed him, there’s nobody else here. We would’ve found them by now if there was,” said Theditor. DClevels ignored Theditors last comment, and they walked back up to the house carrying the deer.


          After dinner was prepared, they all ate quickly. None of them had eaten anything since the day before when they first began the trip. Everyone thought the meal was great… That is, until a sickening green tint began to reveal itself on poor Glenn’s face. “Are you okay Glenn?” Asked Boh. Glenn responded with “Yeah I’m fi-” And then it began. Glenn started throw up, violently and uncontrollably. “Someone help him!” screamed Honey panicking. It was too late though. Glenn began vomiting his internal organs. His liver failed, his heart shut down, and he collapsed to the floor. Dead in a slimy pool of his own blood and guts. “That’s it DC. What did you do to him!” Shouted Theditor with his voice booming like thunder.

~ Nine EE Players soon realizing their fate; One consumed poison, and then there were eight.






Chapter 4: A Bloody Disaster


          “I have no idea what you’re talking about, I didn’t do anything to him!” Yelled DClevels. “How could I have possibly done anything anyways. We’ve been next to each other the whole time!” “Someone had to have done it DC, and you’re looking like a pretty good candidate right now.” Said Theditor. “I don’t know how you did it, I just know that you did it.” Boh then interrupted the argument, “Are you sure you didn’t do it Theditor? You seem to love putting the blame on others.” “Oh shut up Boh. I actually wouldn’t be surprised if you helped DC. You did help with the cooking, correct?” Responded Theditor. Now Failgirl jumped in, “Oh so are you saying I helped kill him also?” “You know what, forget DC. Yes, I think you, Boh, and Honey all did it together.” Said Theditor. “You three all cooked this… this poison.” Theditor then stormed out of the room in a fit of rage.


          “What has gotten into him lately?” Said Honey. “I have not idea,” Answered Reidolol. “Ugh guys, what are we going to do? “Said Failgirl. “One of us is a murderer, and we are all trapped here. We need a plan to insure that nobody else dies.” “I say we all stick together wherever we go, and never let two people be alone together,” Answered Benje00. “We’ll need to change up the room arrangements, now that Glenn is no longer with us. I’m scared to go sleep with Theditor alone… I really think It’s him.” They then proceeded to discuss the room arrangements and they were now changed to this: Honey, Boh, and Failgirl in the first room. DClevels, Gkabyss, and Reidolol, in the second. Then Theditor, and Benje00 each had their own room. “Why are we forced out of the groups?” asked Benje00. Failgirl then spoke, “I’m not too worried about you, but I want Gka and Reid to keep an eye on DC, and Theditor just seems too dangerous to be left with anyone.” “I HEARD THAT!!” Screamed Theditor from the other room. “You just wait until tonight Failgirl. This fail might be your last.” All heads turned towards Theditor, but nobody said anything. Everyone knew exactly what they were thinking. It’s him.


          Night fell once again as the players headed up to their rooms. “Don’t forget to lock your doors,” Reidolol reminded everyone. Voices were heard in room one. “I can’t believe it guys” said Boh. “Theditor is actually going around murdering us…” Honey then spoke, “I hate to say it, but I don’t think it’s him.” Boh and Failgirl both shot Honey a look of disappointment. “Are you kidding me?” They both said in sync. “Did you not hear what he told me,” Said Failgirl. “Yes I heard, but” “But what,” Boh added. “It’s so obvious that it’s him.” “That’s my problem,” said Honey. “It’s too obvious.” “Ugh whatever,” Said an irritated Failgirl. “I’m going downstairs to get a drink, anyone want anything?” “Isn’t that a bit dangerous?” Questioned Boh. “Please, I’ll be down there for five minutes. I don’t think anything will happen to me,” answered Failgirl. “Alright then, be careful said Boh”


          The members of the other rooms were already sound asleep. Theditor was awoken by a sound of movement outside his door. He had a small chill of fear rush through his body. He slowly approached the door, holding a knife that he had taken from the kitchen earlier. He opened the door,but nobody was there. He saw the light on downstairs, and slowly made his way down. He was determined to catch the murderer and clear his name. As he silently reached the bottom of the stairs, he turned to see Failgirl leaning over the sink, with a cup in her hand. “She hasn’t seen me yet,” said a voice inside Theditors head. He cautiously made his way over to her. “This is it, he thought. Time to murder the murderer.” He readied his knife, as if he was about to slit her throat. He was seconds from gathering the nerves to do it, when he glanced to his left and saw an already bloodied knife lying on the floor. He panicked, and quickly turned Failgirl around so that she was facing him. It was then revealed that her throat had already been slit. Blood was rushing down her body. The cup in her hand was filled to the brim with a thick red liquid, and her once white shirt, was now stained red. She was as good as dead. Theditor, scared out of his mind, ran stealthily upstairs. He stopped at the top of the stairs when he heard sounds of laughter coming from the girls room. “so, who’s next?” He heard Honey say.

~ Eight EE Players when the clock struck eleven; One witnessed a fright, and then there were seven.






Chapter 5: Things Get Heated

         
          As Theditor walked back to his room, he was thinking about what he had just heard. “Could it actually be Honey and Boh, working as a team?” “Could that noise outside my door earlier have been them coming back upstairs?” So many questions filled his mind. He knew he needed to talk to someone, but who? Everyone thought he was the murderer. “Hmm” he thought. “There is benje00, he usually seems calm about everything, but wait… He could potentially be the killer as well. He had his own room too. There was nobody to catch him if he snuck out.” Theditor had three strong possibilities for who the murderer could have been. However, he decided to wait until the next morning to bring anything up.


          The clock struck 7, and Reidolol was the first to awaken. He didn’t want to leave the room on his own, so he decided to wake the others up… However, only DC was in the room with him. Reidolol began to panic. He was trapped in the room alone with a potential killer. If he left the room, he would be on his own and could also end up meeting another potential killer. “What I am going to do!” Thought Reidolol.


          DClevels finally woke up about 10 minutes later. “Where’s Gka?” He asked. “I don’t have idea, he were gone when I wake up,” Said Reidolol. “Gone?! Where to?!” said DC. “I not know!” Answered Reidolol. “Please don’t kill me.” “Why would I kill you?” Asked DC. “In about thirty minutes, everyone will know that I was alone with you. If both of you are missing, everyone would think I killed you and Gka. I can’t afford to bring MORE attention to myself.” “Okay… Should go downstair then?” Asked Reid. “Yeah, we might as well. I’ll go wake up the others and meet you down there in a few minutes.” Replied DC. Reidolol began his journey downstairs. After he reached the bottom, a piercing scream blasted it’s way through his mouth. DC, startled by the scream, instantly ran down after him yelling “REIDOLOL! ARE YOU OKAY!?!?” “Yeah I’m fine… but… she’s not, “Said Reidolol pointing at the poor nearly beheaded Failgirl.


          The scream had not only gotten DC’s attention, but others began making there way down the stairs as well. “So,” started Theditor, “Did you girls ever wonder why Failgirl wasn’t in the room with you last night? Or did you plan this.” “Of course we didn’t plan this!” Yelled Boh. “She went downstairs to get a drink of water, and we both fell asleep waiting for her to return,” Answered Honey. “You two fell sleep without checking on her…?” Asked Reidolol. “Well… yeah… we were extremely tired. This stuff gets so stressful, “Said Boh. “you know, last night I overheard you guys saying something,” said Theditor. “What are you talking about?” Questioned Boh. “I heard Honey say, “so, who’s next.”” Then Honey jumped in, “oh we were just talking about what was happening, Boh mentioned a possibility of Gkabyss being the murderer, and I was just thinking out loud.” “Hold on a second.” Said DC. “Has anyone seen Gkabyss today? He was gone when me and Reidolol woke up. He somehow managed to sneak out without us knowing.” “GKA IS GONE TOO!?” Yelled Theditor. “DC, you and Reid have a lot of explaining to do. Wait... where is Reid?” “Oh,” DC replied. “he was in the kitchen, trying to make breakfast for us.” “Some people actually try to be helpful instead of killing people, unlike you Theditor.” They then made their way into the kitchen to find that Reid has mysteriously vanished as well. “WHY DO PEOPLE KEEP DISAPPEARING!!” Screamed Boh. “I don’t understand it! First Shoe, then Gka, and now Reid, not to mention the three deaths of Stage, Glenn, and Fail. WHY IS IT HAPPENING!”


          “At this point it could be anybody,” said Honey. “Theditor and Benje00 both had rooms to themselves, either of them could have snuck out undetected. Gka and Reid are both missing, meaning they could have done it and gone into hiding. Theditor still blames us for it. And we totally overlooked the theory of MrShoe doing it. “Could it really be MrShoe though?” Asked Boh. “Of course it could,” said Theditor. “He probably planned this all along. He forced us to get the magic coin first so he could kill us all off.” “Why would he want to kill us though?” said Boh. “He’s MrShoe, don’t question his terrible logic” Answered Theditor. “Is anyone else getting really hot?” Interrupted Benje00. “Yeah, now that you mention it, I’m starting to sweat,” said Theditor. Everyone else agreed to Theditors Statement. “Someone turn the air conditioning on… If this place even has it.” Said Benje00. “There’s no AC, but we have several fans,” Said Boh. “Quick, turn them on! I’m dying in here,” said Honey.


          Turning on the fans was a terrible idea. After approximately twenty minutes of the fans running at a high speed, a horrible stench of burning flesh began to fill the air. “What is that awful smell?” Asked Boh, trying hard not to breathe through her nose. “No clue,” Responded Theditor, “could it be Failgirls dead corpse? The fans seem to really be spreading the smell out.” Said Benje00. “Oh no…” Said Honey. Everyone turned to face Honey, who was staring at the oven in the kitchen. “What is it?” Asked Benje00. “The temperature displayed on the oven… Someone set it to 900 degrees.” Everyone gasped and Benje00 rushed to turn it off. “Do we dare open it?” Asked Theditor. “Not until it’s cooled off some,” Said Benje00. “We don’t want anyone getting burned.” They waited an hour and then checked back on the oven.


          “who’s going to open it?” Asked Boh. Everyone took a step backwards, except for Theditor. “Ughh fine,” said Theditor. He slowly approached the oven door and opened it cautiously to reveal the charred figure of a human body. Theditor quickly jumped back, horrified. The oven door fell down with a loud bang and now everyone could see what was in it. They all stared at the poor burnt corpse of Reidolol. The last emotion showed on his face was pure terror, as he was slowly cooked alive.

~ Seven EE players looking for food to fix; One chose himself, and then there were six.






Chapter 6: Shot In The Dark


          “Who could have done this,” asked Benje00. Theditor turned to face Benje00 with a weird look on his face, “You.” “What are you talking about?” Questioned Benje00. “How could I have killed Reid?” “Well,” said Theditor, “I don’t remember seeing you this morning until we all moved into the kitchen. You could have easily pulled this off while we were talking in the living room.” “What do you mean? I was standing right behind Honey.” Honey then spoke, “He’s right, he was right with me the whole time… I think it might be Gkabyss.” “I don’t know,” said Theditor. “I still think it’s Benje00. Gka doesn’t seem like the type to commit all these murders.” “And I do?” Asked Benje00. Theditor ignored Benje00’s last comment and turned to DClevels. “And there’s also you. Both of your roommates from last night vanished today. “I didn’t do anything to them,” said DClevels. “Oh really?” Said Theditor. “How is it that you were the only one to see reid before he died.” “I saw Reid before he died,” said Benje00, trying to help out DC. “I looked in the kitchen before joining you guys in the living room.” Theditor spoke once again, “so it was you then!” Benje00 now looked irritated. “Seeing him does not mean that I killed him!” “Guys can we stop fighting for once?!” Yelled Honey. “If we actually worked together we could figure this out.” “I agree with Honey,” Said DClevels, you need to stop Theditor.” “Well, I’ll help you guys out as soon as I get back from using the restroom,” said Boh. “Oh you aren’t going anywhere!” Shouted Theditor. “Nobody is allowed to leave the group, we are all staying together from now on.”


          “Do you honestly still think that I’m the one killing people?” Said Boh. “I have no idea, but I know that if you leave the group, you’ll probably end up dead like everyone else,” stated Theditor. “You know, I wouldn’t be surprised if MrShoe is behind this,” said DC. Honey then spoke, “Why do you say that?” “Don’t you find it odd that as soon as he got back from his “vacation”, he created a contest that lead to us being trapped here? Asked DClevels. “He probably rigged this whole thing to kill us.” “You know, you could be right,” said Boh. “In fact, what if he’s watching us right now.” “How could he possibly be watching us?” Questioned Benje00. “He said he had a map of the island, I’m sure there are some hidden passages throughout the house that he could be using,” Answered Boh. “You must be going crazy Boh, there aren’t any secret passages here,” said Theditor. “I would have found them if there were.” “Ughhh I can’t take this horrible smell anymore!” Yelled Honeybuckles. “I’m getting as far away from here as I can, you guys can either come with me or stay.” The group of five then left the kitchen, and journeyed down into the basement, where the smell would never reach them.


          “Has anyone even gone down here yet?” Asked Boh nervously. “Not that I know of,” answered Benje00. “We should be careful then,” said Boh. “If my theory of MrShoe sneaking through hidden passages is right, he could have set up some traps down here.” Theditor burst into laughter. “Are you serious? You really think he’s setting up traps to kill us?” “Leave her alone,” said DClevels, with an angry look on his face. “Why should I? For all I know, she could be the murderer,” stated Theditor. “CAN YOU JUST STOP!” Screamed DClevels. Theditor turned to DC and took a step towards him while saying, “Stop what? Trying to keep people alive?” “No, stop accusing everyone of being the murderer,” said DClevels.” “Actually I find it funny how you’ve probably accused the murderer of murdering, and you’re still alive. Maybe it’s you?” “Theditor then took another step towards DC, and smacked him right across the face. DClevels collapsed to the floor clutching his left cheek, as Benje00 ran over to help him up.


          “GUYS STOP FIGHTING,” Yelled Boh, keeping her distance from Theditor. “Stay out of this Boh, unless you want to get hurt as well!” roared Theditor. Theditor then turned back towards DC and Benje00, and was instantly punched in the stomach by DC. Theditor nearly fell over backwards, but managed to keep his balance. He then punched DC directly between his eyes. DC fell to the ground once again, as Benje00 ran at Theditor, trying to break up the fight. Theditor made an attempt to throw Benje00 to the ground, but Benje00 managed to grab Theditor’s arm, and slammed him down instead. Just as Theditor hit the ground, DClevels got back to his feet, and went for his chance for revenge on Theditor. DClevels skillfully dodged Benje00’s attempts at keeping him away, and reached Theditor just has he was getting up. He then proceeded to kick Theditor square in the face, and with a loud crack, Theditor was sent backwards onto his back. Benje00 ran to pull DC off of Theditor before anymore damaged was caused to either of them.


          Theditor slowly sat up, and blood immediately began to pour out of his nose. “YOU BROKE MY NOSE!” Screamed Theditor,” full of rage. DClevels slowly backed away from Theditor, as he approached him with a look of insanity on his face. “You broke my nose… And now I’m going to break you,” said Theditor.” As he continued walking towards DClevels. Benje00, being brave, once again attempted to stop Theditors insane actions. He raced towards them and stood directly between Theditor and DClevels. “If you want to kill him, you’re going to have to kill me too,” said Benje00. Theditor ignored Benje00, and quickly shoved him to floor. He continued towards DC once again, and finally reached him. DClevels finally spoke. “Fine go ahead and kill me. Then everyone will know the truth, that you are the murderer. They will finally be able to put an end to your madness.” Theditor thought about this for a quick second, and then proceeded to push DClevels backwards. A soft click was heard, as DC backed up over a small line of wire. DClevels fell over, and before he even hit the ground, an arrow had shot directly through his left ear, and out his right ear. He hit the ground with a thud, as everyone in the room gasped and stared at Theditor in horror. There was no hope left for DClevels. He now had an arrow implanted in his skull. He was dead, and Theditor, was responsible.


          Honey and Boh walked towards Benje00, and the three of them stood there, staring at Theditor. “I didn’t know…” Started Theditor, but he knew nobody would believe him. “Well,” said Boh. “I think you need to be locked away from the rest of us until we get off of this island. Honeybuckles and Benje00 both agreed. The three of them walked Theditor upstairs to his room. Normally he would fight against them, but in this case, he was far too devastated to argue. This was the third death that he had seen up close. Once they reached Theditors room, Honey opened the door, while Benje00 shoved Theditor inside. Honey then closed the door, and Boh locked it, assuring that Theditor would remain trapped, and unable to harm anyone else.

~ Six EE players, trying to stay alive. One destroyed his hearing, and then there were five.






Chapter 7: A New Path Unfolds


          Night slowly fell once again as Theditor sat alone in his room, thinking about what had happened that day. Thinking about how he swore DC had been behind it all.
