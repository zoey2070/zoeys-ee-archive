KOcrew History and Level Library


History of KOcrew:
KOcrew was formed back in 2011 by the two users, Master1 and Insan3editor. After their previous failed attempt to make a successful crew (IBM) they decided to give it another shot. After forming the crew, many friends of theirs from the beta-only club were hired, these include: Jak, Matt, Showpath, Kaylee123, Garfield2436, and more. As the crew finished up the first level, Pirate Escape, Insan3editor slowly stopped showing up on Everybody Edits. After eventually realizing that a replacement was needed, Jak, who was the first one to join the crew was upgraded to Co-leader.


At this point, the crew was going pretty well, the crew's most famous map of the time "Cursed Minis" was released, along with several other maps: Super Stairs, Escape the Cliff, Ice Co op, and Adventure. Several other awesome users were also picked up at this time, Vulcan, Muftwin, Aoitenshi, Polis, 0176, Naztyrushz, Express50, and more! This gave the crew the people needed to create even more maps! In no time at all (several months) four more maps were released under the KO title. Elements, Hellish Escape, Enter Da Darkness (Mostly a Kaylee123/Master1 colab), and Ice climb (Mostly an Aoitenshi world). Also, the Halloween contest had come and gone, awarding Matt, Jak, 0176, and Garfield2436, a 2nd place prize, with there outstanding map "Sinister"


More time passed, and KOcrew finally released a new world after a long period of no worlds, Cursed Minis 2. Around the same time, Jak had forced Gkabyssflorida into the crew without talking to Master1 about it first but Master1 decided to be nice and allowed Gka to join - not knowing how amazing of a member he would turn out to be. Other new members that joined around this time include: Buzzerbee, Thuggishprune, Failgirl101, Tak4n, Pyromaniac, Theditor, Stagecrew, Deetz, and DClevels. After all these people had joined, Space Escape, and a revamped version of Escape the Cliff were soon released.


KOcrew began to die down once again. Jak had quit EE, and another co leader was needed once again. Matt and Gkabyssflorida were both amazing options at the time, but in the end, Matt was chosen for the spot, and Gkabyssflorida became a crew Admin. More new members joined, including: Boh, 1nsanity, Itsmeandersonlol, Addi, Killingpepper123, Skyfalcon, Shadowboy, Legoking2000, Blindz0r, and P788. Around the same time frame, KOcrew released Death Dominion, and Midnight Kothedral. And then nothing was ever spoken about KOcrew again.


...Until now, when Master1 decided to finish one final world and call quits for everything. 222Hockey222, and Forcesniper were the final 2 members to join, and the final world created by KOcrew was "Caves Are So 2011"
