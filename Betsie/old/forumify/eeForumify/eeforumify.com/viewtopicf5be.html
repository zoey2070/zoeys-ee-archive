Get your suggestions noticed!

Do not post your suggestions in this thread, put them on the suggestion site! We will not be checking this thread for suggestions!

I've noticed often that good ideas are lost in the floods of duplicates. This is your chance to really get your ideas noticed and added to EE!

Please visit our UserVoice page, to submit your suggestions and vote on others.

While submitting suggestions, please keep the following rules in mind:

1. No duplicate ideas. We don't want to see the same thing a million times.
2. Only one idea per post. Seriously. Compound ideas will be marked as "declined" without a second thought.
3. Use your votes sparingly! You've got a limited number, so don't waste them on every little cool idea.
4. Suggestions only. Bug Reports can go here. Don't post your levels.
5. Be descriptive in your post body. We aren't mind readers!
6. Don't spam. You'll be blocked from posting actual suggestions, and you probably don't want that.
7. Be constructive when giving feedback on other people's suggestions. Yes, some will probably be pretty stupid, but you don't need to tell them that.
8. Game & Graphics suggestions only. Don't suggest things for individual levels, or the forums, or new hairstyles you think I should really try out.
9. Be concise. Short, sweet, and to the point.
10. Present your ideas professionally. We probably wouldn't even look at an idea called "lol add this". Also; spelling. Please use it.

When voting on suggestions, please keep the following rules in mind:

1. The system automatically tries to remove spam votes
2. I go through manually and remove suspicious votes
3. You're only hurting the chances of the one you've spam-voted up

These rules are subject to change as people will likely find loopholes or do things I haven't thought of, so check back often.

Chris has agreed to strongly consider the 10 best suggestions monthly! Think hard about your post before submitting it!
