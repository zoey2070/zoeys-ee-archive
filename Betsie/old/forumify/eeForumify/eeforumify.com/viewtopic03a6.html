Section I - Introduction To OKcrew



OKcrew, as said in the title of the topic, is a sequel to KOcrew which was created by Master1 and Insan3editor back in 2011. Since KOcrew was so old when it began, many of the levels are now bad by todays standards. This crew is sort of a way to start over and eliminate those bad worlds from our level list, without destroying the crews history. Old KOcrew members are free to join if they desire to, and we will also be recruiting new members! World creation will begin in a few days, so scroll down if you are interested in joining!
