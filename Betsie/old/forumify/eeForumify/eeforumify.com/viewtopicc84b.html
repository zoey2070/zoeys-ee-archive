Everybody Edits Event 2010/11: Post your levels here!

CLOSED

Since there's only a couple of days left until your time is up, I figured that you guys could start posting your levels in this topic if you've already finished them and feel that they are complete.

Every registered team is allowed to submit their level until 1 January 00:00 GMT (Time is subject to change based on our schedules) or else it will not be accepted. Once the level links have been posted, Chris will then copy all of the levels to a private server for us judges to use for testing them out and deciding which 10 maps will survive until the final voting. So please make sure that your level is online and waiting for us by then, veeery important!

When posting your level, make sure your post follows this structure:

#01. Team name (Or username if you're by yourself)
Ingame usernames: dude1, dude2, etc...
"Level Name"
http://everybodyedits.com/games/Your-level-link

So start posting your links to your levels, and good luck with the contest.

NOTE: Any posts that do not post team information or level links will be deleted. Same restrictions apply to users who are not signed up for the contest.
