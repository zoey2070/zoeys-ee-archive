My Saved Worlds Series

Hi, you're probably thinking this is a thread where I'll post my levels. No. Welcome to...


My Trolled Saved World

My Trolled Saved World (Beta)



This is my 4th small saved world. I didn't know what to do, so I did something like EX Crew's level... I trolled it, and then I made a path. Hope you like it!

Difficulty: 5 (Easy)

Coins: 3



My Empty Saved World

My Empty Saved World (Beta)


Another one to My Saved Worlds Series! Not really hard, but not boring. I didn't put a crown because... Well, it's my empty saved world, after all. Hope you like it!

Difficulty: 7 (Medium)

Coins: 2



My Colored Saved World


My Colored Saved World (Beta)


I was thinking of making a rainbow spiral, but then this came out, and I liked it. Also, not hard. I admit this one is not so good as it's not too fun, except a bit the last part.

Difficulty: 3 (Very easy)

Coins: 3


My RGB Saved World


My RGB Saved World (Beta)


I thought it was a good idea to make a doors level, so I made it. This one is easy too... I'm trying to make them hard, but I can't (I can, but not if I put a theme before making the level). This one wasn't easy to do, but I finally did it. Hope you like it!

Difficulty: 6 (Easy-Medium)

Coins: 10


My Wavin Saved World


My Wavin Saved World (Beta)


My first coin maze. I personally like it, even if it isnt reaaaally hard. It was not really easy to do it, though. In fact, I was a while. At first I trolled with a line. Then I cleared it up, then I made the minigames, then I cleared them up.

Difficulty: 7 (Medium)

Coins: 4


My Square Saved World


My Square Saved World (Beta)


I loved doing this one. It was fun to do it and test it, and play it over and over again, until I could go through every single mini. Also, as its colorful, it makes you happy!

Difficulty: 6 (Easy-Medium)

Coins: 4


My Spiral Saved World


My Spiral Saved World (Beta)


One of the levels I did with TakoMan, the winner of the contest. The minis arent really hard as theyre all in gates and coin doors, but, Freddie Mercury was awesome.

Difficulty: 4 (Very easy-Easy)

Coins: 2


My Alive Saved World


My Alive Saved World (Beta)


The other level I did with TakoMan. This ones a bit harder, as its all done with secret blocks. I have to admit both image idea and level idea came from TakoMan in this level. If you start above, you have to get all 5 coins, go below, and get all below 5 coins, and vice versa.

Difficulty: 7 (Medium)

Coins: 10


My Ninja Saved World


My Ninja Saved World (Beta)


Well, first I got the idea of making a rounded square. So I made the rounded edges. Then I made the middle circle. Then I divided it in four. Then I made the minigames. Then I put the coins. Then I put the crown. Easy peasy.

Difficulty: 8 (Medium-Hard)

Coins: 4



My Candy Saved World

My Candy Saved World (Beta)


As I said in the post, tomorrow (2012/01/14) I'm travelling tomorrow (well, actually today, becuase it's already 3 a.m. here lol), so, just because I'm nice I decided to make a last level before leaving. So, there you got it, My Candy Saved World. It's pretty short and it's the first one to have any portals or backgrounds. Also pretty easy, though a bit annoying to fall and have to start over.

Difficulty: 3-4 (Very easy-Easy)

Coins: 4



Note: You can use levels which have 5 coins or less for Lopoi's Upgrade City. Total number of coins you can get: 26
