Cursed Potion Minigames

The rules are basic: I use a cursed potion and I try one player tocuhes my curse effect. The player that respawns,gets a point. The 5 players that have the highest number of points will be in the list of the top: "Top 5 best players". Read the other rules of the right corner at the top of the world.
There are 3 maps in this version of the world: Farm,Moon and Sea. You can choose the map you want to play.

Some questions of the game:

- Can I use my own cursed potion? You can only use your own cursed potions if I have not cursed potions.

- What happens if I don't do the things that say the rules of the roght corner of the map? You will be kicked.

- How can I choose the map I want to play? You can choose it by saying "1","2" or "3".

- What map will you choose if some players say different numbers? I'll choose the number that the first player said.

- How do I win? As I said before, you must get killed by the curse to win points.
