KilleRabbit X-Mas

You came to Tom's house to enjoy Christmas party.
Play Beta!
Play Normal!

Credits:

Matt: Translation
Kiraninja: Title logo
Nongtal: Assist

Thanks for TOOP and Badoosh for the idea.

Walk-Through (Please push ctrl+A)

-Will you skip opening?
Answer: No. or Yes. Either is Okay. (If you want to watch Ending 1, Please answer "No.")
   -No
   Cathy runs to out of Tom's house. Will you stop her?
      -No: Ending 1.

When game has began,
   -Hangs about at home until 6 minutes.
   -> Ending 2

-Goes to the bathroom
      -You: Lock the door
         -You: Can you close the windows?
            -You led Bob safely. If you want to watch Ending 3, Please hangs about at home until 11 minutes.

-After 4 minutes past,
   -Talks to Tom. (He is in the living room.)
      -You: How are you?
         -You: I need a medicine.
            -Laxative
               -You got Laxative.
   -Talks to Bob when you have a laxative. (He is in the bathroom.)
      -You: Yes.
         -Yes
            -You got a bolt.
   -Talks to Mary when you have a bolt. (She is in the second floor.)
      -You: Can I help you?
         -You: Yes. Here you are.
            -You led Mary safely. If you want to watch Ending 4, Please hangs about at home until 21 minutes.

-After 11 minutes past,
   -Talks to Mary. (She is in the second floor.)
      -You: What's wrong?
         -You got a machine.
   -Talks to Cathy (She is hiding at the kitchen. Please find her.)
      -You: I'm the killer. (If you don't have a machine, You have to watch Ending 4.)
         -You repair a blackout. If you want to watch Ending 5, Please hangs about at home until 21 minutes.

-After a blackout repaired,
   -Talks to Tom. (He is in the living room.)
      -Gives Tom the machine.
         -You: No
            -You: Yes
               -You: The toilet
                  - You led Tom and Mary safely.

-After talking to Tom,
   -Goes to the lobby.
      -Talks to the man.
         -Uses the machine: Ending 7.
         -Uses the gun: Ending 8.
         -Runs to outside: Best Ending.

-How to go to Ending 6?
   -Goes to the exit
      -Open the door.
