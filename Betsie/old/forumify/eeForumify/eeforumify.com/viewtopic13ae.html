Montezuma II

The third level of The Many Appearances of S17 - Montezuma II.



Here you are, once again at a random time in history. Somehow, this time the journey felt longer. This level was started a really long time ago, and I wasn't as good at designing levels back then as I am now. So that's why this level kinda sucks a bit. This level is slightly different to the others in the series. In the other levels, the path that the player takes is the actual path that the character in the story takes. In this level the path the player takes is more like what the character observes. A bit more of the story has been revealed in this level, so see if you can figure out the story behind S17.
