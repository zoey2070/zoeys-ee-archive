The first Everybody Edits event ever: NEW YEARS 2011!

That's right! Get ready for...

We have decided to make the end of 2010 as fun as possible for all the Everbody Edits players out there by announcing the very first Everbody Edits Event ever! If you feel that you've got what it takes to reap the grand prize, then by all means, sign up!

And the theme of this contest is... NEW YEARS EVE 2011!

What this means is that the level MUST be revolve around New Years Eve 2011, or else it will not qualify for this contest. Once the contest ends 31 december, we will open up votes so that the users and players can vote for their favorite level based on originality, design and fun factor. So take your best shots and make the single most epic Everybody Edits level ever made!

PRIZES:

1st place: 1000 max energy to every player in the winning team!

2nd place: 500 max energy to every player in the winning team!

3rd place: 250 max energy to every player in the winning team!

TIMEFRAME:

23 - 25 december: Enter your teams! (Signing your team MUST be in this topic)

25 - 31 december: Create your levels!

1 - 7 january: The voting begins!

RULES:

- You MUST sign up your team before the 25th of december, or else you will not qualify for the contest.

- Only 5 creators per team at most. You are however free to be only one creator, if you wish to do so.

- The completed map MUST be New Years-themed or else it will be disqualified.

- Being in more than 1 team is NOT allowed.

- Animated/autobuilded/imported levels will instantly be disqualified.

- Make sure that you're making the level on a world that can be accessed by non-betas as well.

---
If anyone has any questions, feel free to post them in this topic as well as entering your team. We in the Everybody Edits staff are thrilled to see what kinds of creative levels you guys can come up with during this following week and we hope that this is an experience that will reward you guys just as much as it rewards us giving you this opportunity.

Have fun!
