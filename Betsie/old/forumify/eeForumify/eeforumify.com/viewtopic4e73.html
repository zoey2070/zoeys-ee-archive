Re: What is WorldKey for?

Chris originally implemented bldatam to prevent tools like EEAnimator from sending block packets.

The idea was to have the client get information from the first packet on join that is unique to the world, which would in-turn affect the packets to be 'unique'.
Chris didn't really understand how to make it truly unique however so it was easily evaded.

The same applies to people spamming keys.
