HookCounter [ON/OFF]

I was bored and made a level that keeps track of how many hook jumps you do via bot.
You have the option to do 1x1 hooks or 1x2 hooks.
The level is surprisingly addicting.

Made in CupCake, so special thanks to Processor.
Also special thanks to UGotPwned for helping me clean up the code.

To find out your stats say "!stats" and to find out another player's stats say "!stats <player>"

I'm too tired and lazy to make a more elaborate topic right now.

Anyway, here is the link:

http://everybodyedits.com/games/PWBzHkuu21bkI
