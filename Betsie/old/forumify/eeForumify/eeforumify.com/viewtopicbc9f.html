Everybody Edits Halloween 2011: Submit your levels here!

Halloween is barely a week away and it's finally time for everyone to start submitting your levels. Every registered team is allowed to submit their level until 8 November 00:00 GMT (Time is subject to change based on our schedules) or else it will not be accepted. Once the level links have been posted, Chris will then copy all of the levels to a private server for the judges to use for testing them out and deciding which levels will win the grand prizes.

VERY IMPORTANT: Chris has decided to not let you edit out any flaws in the level once the level has been copied over for judging so make sure that your level is 100 % functional and ready to play.

When posting your level, make sure your post follows this syntax:

#1. Team name
Members: dude1, dude2, etc...
"Level Name"
http://everybodyedits.com/games/Your-level-link

DO NOT MAKE ANY POSTS THAT DON'T CONTAIN THE TEMPLATE ABOVE. IT WILL BE CONSIDERED SPAM.

Any private questions relating to the contest can be answered via PM. Best of luck to all the teams that have signed up!
