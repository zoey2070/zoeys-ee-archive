If it is advertised on Twitch, Mr. Shoe should clean up the lobby to make a good impression on newer players.

- There will be an influx of signups, and they will begin at level one. Many rooms do not allow these people, and could have a bad taste (I just signed up, and I was kicked from a room!).
- The signup might suggest "check your spam filter" because email companies might think that it is a spam because of a sudden surge of signups
- The lobby should have popular levels at the top (for just that period). This will give the newer players something to do, and then while they learn the game they can determine which levels are good and which ones are bad. The popular levels should be very easy, because these are people who have basically never played the game before.
- Introduce referrals; people get gems and such if someone else signs up using a link and decides to buy gems
