Block Crush

Welcome to Block Crush!

Info
Block crush is a game made in EE made by me and Reidolol. It’s actually a remake (NOT a copy) of Candy Crush Saga.
P.S. It’s NOT a bot! (99% of people that came to the block crush world asked if this is a bot)

Links
Main world
Levels world

Instructions

Swap two blocks to make matches of 3 and crush blocks.

Match 4 horizontally to make a vertical bomb.

Match 4 vertically to make a horizontal bomb.

Match blocks in the shape of a “T” or an “L” to make a 3x3 bomb.

Match 5 straight blocks to make a color bomb. Swap the color bomb with any color to destroy all blocks of the same color in the screen.

Mixing a horizontal and vertical bomb sets both off.

Mixing a horizontal/vertical bomb with a color bomb makes all the blocks of the same color horizontal/vertical bombs and sets them off.

Mixing a horizontal/vertical bomb with a 3x3 bomb makes a 3x3 block that explodes like a giant horizontal and a vertical bomb.

Blocks
Normal blocks: They have nothing special.
Horizontal bombs: When it gets crushed, it crushes the whole row
Vertical bombs: When it gets crushed, it crushes the whole column.
3x3 bombs: When it gets crushed, it explodes and crushes the nearest blocks.
Color bombs: When swapped with another block, it crushes all the blocks with the same color as the one swapped.

Blockers
Break blocks: Do matches adjacent to them to clear them. 
Locks: You can’t move the locked block, but can unlock it by clearing it with a match of the block behind it or exploding.
Chocolate: Grows if not cleared in a move. Clear it by doing matches adjacent to it.
Block swirls: Like break blocks, but movable.
Time bombs: You have a certain amount of moves to clear them, or they blow up and you lose.
Chocolate spawners: Spawn chocolate everytime it (chocolate) is not cleared.
Double break blocks: Name says it all.
Triple break blocks: Name says it all again.
Quadruple break blocks: Name says it all yet another time.
Zok: traps special blocks (usually) there, clear matches adjacent to it OR match with the block below it to remove it.
Quintuple break blocks: Name says it all for the last time ever.
Big cube bomb, clear all its parts by making matches adjacent to it to clear it and make all blocks explode.
Square tornadoes: every 2 moves, they move to a random block and they leave a crack when they move to another block. The crack dissapears after 1 move.
Chest: Break the key by matching it with the same color to break a layer of the chest.
Popblocks: Hit them with a horizontal/vertical bomb so they grow,  if you hit it for the 3rd time, it explodes into a color bomb.

 

Level types
Moves: Get enough points in the number of moves.
Jelly: Clear all the jelly.
Ingredient: Get all blocks and cubes to the bottom.
Timed: Get enough points in the time limit.
Block Order: Break the ordered blocks in the move limit.

Worlds
Block world: The main world and first one you play.
BG world: Unlocked in Block world level 50, this world features BG scale.
Fine world: Unlocked in BG world level 50, this world features fine meter.
Insane world: Unlocked after completing the whole block world, this world has all levels with insanely hard difficulty. (No, it doesn’t mean it has only the levels with insanely hard difficulty, all levels are turned into insanely hard)

Features

BG scale
BG Scale: It is the main feature of the BG world. Two random colors will appear on the sides, and you must balance the BG scale by clearing the same amount of both colors. The yellow thing filling at the bottom is the BG meter, when it fills, a BG struck will happen, which will clear one of the colors on the BG scale for as long as the BG struck lasts.

Fine Meter
Fine meter: It is the main feature of the Fine world. When you break a block, you will get points on the color you broke, when you reach the top, all the blocks of that color will explode and fine meter will be reseted. However, if you don’t break a block for a certain ammount of moves/time, you will lose.

Teleporters
Teleporters: They first appear in level 66, what they do is that when a block falls through it and there is space from the other side (where the teleporter leads to), it will fall through the teleporter and fall in where it leads.

Conveyor Belts
Conveyor Belts: They first appear in level 81, They move every move, and they move all the blocks that are on top of it too. The only blockers it could move are the two movable blockers: Time bombs and block swirls.

Zokfish
They appear during a Block Disaster on Jelly levels, but from level 425 they can appear on the board, but covered with zoks. What they do is, go out of the screen and bring THREE of them instead. and hit a random block. If it’s the same color as the zokfish, then you get 1020 points for each. otherwise, 240

Frog
Frog: First appears in level 606. Feed it with blocks of its color, until you can use him to explode a 3x3 square. Then he changes color.
