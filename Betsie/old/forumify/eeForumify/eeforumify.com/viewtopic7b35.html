2014 Art Festival

Hello my fellow community. I will be hosting a 2014 Art Festival for Everybody Edits. This is an event to express yourself. The event will be held for 3 days, after which the results will be posted on the forums for all to see. The rules of the event are posted in the room. Also I will post a general rule set here.

1: Do not interrupt someone else's box. The boxes are 30x30. You may us any space in THAT box.
2: Do not use more then one box. I am not expecting a whole lot to show up, but if there are empty box's please leave them open for any late entries.
3: Please be respectful, no profanity, no slang or inappropriate words. Nothing offensive.

The art dose not have to be just visual, it may be mini levels, music, activities, or anything of the sort. If you do not want your art posted on the forums you may choose not too, just mark the corner of your box with red and it will not be made public.

I hope to see you all there are the 2014 festival.
