EE Comics

Tired of trying to create an interactive story in EE?

Here's a solution: Comics!

In addition to occupying less space, you will have more tranquility to explain what is happening in the story and will be able to draw a small simulation of that.

How to make comics:

Step 1: Squares

You can choose the size of each square, the squares do not need all of equal size, and can be any size, but remember: The bigger, takes up more space, but you can add more detail to the scene.

Step 2: Scenes and characters

You choose where the story is presented, who the characters are and what's happening are.

Step 3: Signs

You can write some actions or dialogue on the boards, so it helps who is viewing to know what is happening.

You can also make different designs.

Comics is rarely used, imagine if they were widely used.
