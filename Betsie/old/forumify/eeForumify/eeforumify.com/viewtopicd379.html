Re: ee mathamatics 2

Step 1: Keep a spike somewhere in the bottommost corner. This can just get you back to the top, allowing more coins
Step 2: One line of block/gravity/whatever only. All else you can fall down.
Step 3: Strategies listed here, (which the corresponding number of blocks used in total).
Strategy 1:
Boosting back to the top (99 blocks). By holding right when (re)spawning you'll get to the 2nd boost, and the others can be reached by boosting slowly/quickly. This is a simple solution because, with the extra running boosts, you don't always need 1 every 4 blocks, but it is hard to calculate out.
Strategy 2:
Direct jump (~19 blocks). Assuming the "player" is a EE god (not god mode) who knows how to reach the hardest-to reach areas, we can simplify it into a hold space+right thing. For all other blocks you can simply stop at the column and fall down.
However, there are blind spots.

The earliest spots can easily be filled by stopped RIGHT at the block and jump again. Then everything should be perfect.

Not sure if there were other ways tho. The best solution I found is 118,585.
