Dragon Quest

You are a new born dragon. Since new born dragons are not very powerful you decide you must complete the 5 dragon tasks to become stronger. This is dragon quest. Can you become LEGENDARY DRAGON?


http://everybodyedits.com/games/PWh1f16uLMbUI
Cool info about the level:
-I started this level a long time ago when i decided to build with a bunch of random people, we decided to draw a bunch of dragons. I think the green one is mine i dont remember who drew the rest.
-This level uses a Low Gravity world.
-There is a bonus blue coin in each of the trials, if you miss it you can use the warp at the end to go back. You can get a crown if you collect them all.

Be sure to post what level dragon you are when you are finished playing.
