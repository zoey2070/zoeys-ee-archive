MAGIC COINS; all the information you need to know!

Zoey's topic wrote:

What is a magic coin?
 Looks like a normal gold coin
 Appears randomly
 Message pops up when getting it
 Can get a lot
 You will get a magic coin before dieing of a shark attack.
 Two types, 4 subtypes
 Gold coins only

How do I get it?
 Wander around a level collecting gold coins
 Hack EE
 Join a coin farm level and go afk
 Join a coin regen level and go afk
 Just generally collect gold coins
 Anyone can get them
→ Except Zoey2070

What do I get from it?
 Energy
→ +1 max energy
→ +2 max energy
→ +10 max energy
→ +15 max energy
 A wizard smiley.
 One coin = one reward
 Rewards are completely unrelated to each other

How do I know when I get it?
 Message pops up in chat
 Message pop up in-game, too.

*This topic is current undergoing re-construction it will include in the near future;- detection, how to obtain, how to increase chances to obtain, myths, and anything related to MAGIC COINS.


This is worth your time in reading; to help this investigation please submit how you have achieved obtaining a "magic coin" after the updates.

After the magic coin update, I have come to these conclusions;
-Magic coin generators, do work but your chances are a lot lower. (Sample test of over 10 hours, plus posts in topic)
-Magic coin farms do not work, these are the ones where there is a key and you drop every few seconds (Sample test of over 15 hours+posts in blog)
-World population has absolutely no effect on your chances of getting a coin, neither does the amount of coins in that one giving world.

My theories on how the detection works;
-Worlds can be marked by moderators to no award magic coins to players in that current world.
-Worlds can be automatically marked by a system when a world says "magic" "coin" "farm" "generator" to not award magic coins to players in that current world.
-Being "afk" or not moving or touching buttons will stop you from being awarded magic coins.
-Coins that have been created for less then "x" amount of time will not have any chance of being a magic coin; whether that "x" is 1minute, 2 and so on, I will probably never know.

My theories on how to increase your chances;
-Move a lot, I don't know why but I have scored coins very quickly on random maps (race maps) that require a lot of moving and attention.
-Do not join maps with "magic" "coin" "farm" "generator" or any relevant words.

Extra true facts;
-Maps with a lot of coins, or lines of coins do award you with magic coins, I have been awarded twice in one of these maps it had lines of coins.
-The population of the map has absolutely no effect on whether or not you will get a coin or not.
-Star maps, saved worlds, etc. will not effect your chances in any way.
-Personally with self experience, maps with little coins have a lower chance of awarding you a magic coin compared to maps with a lot.

Still to test;
-Spamming keyboard movements with an script on a coinfarming world, and non coin farming world.
-Making a coin farm/generator world without the words "magic" "coin" "farm" "generator" in the worlds name.
-Spamming coins on a map by myself and collecting them with godmode.

As I have tested this a lot I would like to see what your thoughts on my ideas are, please also tell us how you have obtained a magic coin, what kind of map, and how fast it was. and any relevant information.
