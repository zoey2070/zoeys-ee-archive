The Level Library.

Have you ever been looking for a good level to play but cannot find one and you go back to playing one you've already played? Or just been so bored you'll play anything? The Level Library is a mission to round up all the good levels and place them here, in alphabetical order. These levels will either be decided by a judge, or the community. Please read the FAQ to learn more. I will begin putting levels in tomorrow.

DO NOT QUOTE THIS POST

-----Other Help Needed for the Level Library.-----


- I currently need more judges! MAX; 10. For now.

- I really need some levels!  I can't get them all!

- Any suggestions for this thread are welcomed.

- Proofreaders, People to look through the posts to find mistakes. All levels have- Link, Screenshot,BY "such and such" unless it's a crew map, and have a dotted lime after it.[/center]

(The level library is nowhere close to being complete, please help!)

-----Frequently Asked Questions-----

What is the Level Library?

A collection of levels that the community considers good, and are rounded up in a topic to show the community more levels and have fun.

What is a Judge, and how do I become one?

A judge is someone who basically says if a level is in or not, right away.  You can become one by contacting me via P.M. showing me that you've beat plenty of "hard" levels or show me that you have ability to make "good" levels.

How can the community decide to put a level in the library that the judges don't want?

First, you must have the mini-map, links, and authors of the map posted and then five people must vote a map into the Library. The user must have above 50 posts on the forums.

Are any world size allowed?

Yes, any size is allowed.

My Crew's Level starts with an "A" and I can't find it in "A", why is this?

I'm putting all crew levels together in the letter that the crew starts with. Thus if it is MG Crew, it all goes under "M" [/center]

How exactly can I help?

You can contact me to become a judge, or recommend levels through here and if a judge likes them or it gets five votes, it's in. That or you can do the other things mentioned at the top under the introduction paragraph.
