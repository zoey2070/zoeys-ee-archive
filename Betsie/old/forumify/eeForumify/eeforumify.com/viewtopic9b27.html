Trolling Tips and Techniques
How to be a good troll?

Every committed TI² has a technique to get the edit permission or the code, then trolololo.
Even after getting the code, trolling can fail. If you ever wondered how to make your trolling abilities better, read this.

How to get the code?
It is important to remember that you will have more chance getting the code in worlds called "Code is xxxx", "Boss/Stairs/Minis/Art to code", "Help me please", "Helpers needed" etc.
Except for hacking the code (with code crackers, programs that reveal the minimap or modified clients), there are many ways to get the edit permissions or the code.

1. Wear a rare smiley. Authors that give the code really like people with rare smileys. They feel that people with rare smileys will be better at making art or bossing.

Examples of rare smileys wrote:

Ultimate Fan (Fanboy, to make them feel like you are very committed to the game), any of the beta smileys (preferably cool shades dude, so it makes them feel you are important and serious), Big Spender (will ultimately give you the code every time, because money makes a person better), Christmas/New Years 2010/Valentine's/Grinch (To make them feel like you are experienced in the game), Witch/Fire Wizard/Wizard (to make them feel like you play a lot) and Contest reward smileys like the bird/pumpkin/bunny smileys

2. Pretend you really enjoy the level, and interested in it, and gain trust. It also helps to woot the level (before trolling to gain trust or after trolling to make them think you didn't troll)

You: Best smiley art I have ever seen.
You: I like Copy Bosses, they are very creative.
You: This boss is very fair and not random.
You: Your stairs are the best among all identical stair levels.

Showing interest in this kind of levels proves that you are a serious player.

3. Ask for the code.

You: I like this level very much, can I help with it please?
You: I have a unique smiley idea that I'd like to try. Can you let me do it?
You: I am a very experienced bosser and I play Everybody Edits just for doing that. Can you let me boss?
You: Can I edit?
You: Code?

How to Troll Better?
After you get the code, you are probably wondering what are the best tips for making a troll a good troll.

1. Wear the robber/ninja smiley so you are invisible in the minimap.
2. Misdirect them. Owners and helpers tend to follow the troll wherever it goes. Use it! Move the cursor to the right while going to the left, that will mislead them and you will keep trololololoing for a few more good moments.
3. Invite a friend to help you troll. you can find friends in the friendlist, Skype, Facebook, TeamViewer and the forums.
4. Do not put the cursor in the middle of the screen or surround yourself with it while trolling, because that will reveal you. If you can, it is recommended to even surround another user so he will look like he's the troll. When you troll and make the owner kick another user, you become a Double Troll.
5. Do not troll in empty parts of the map if you didn't troll the playable or artistic part of it. If you did, feel free to take the level to the next level and fill it with the art of troll.
6. Always blame another player for trolling.
7. It is already suggested to gain trust. If you hold on a bit before trolling and gain trust, that will make the owner of the level trust you. Then you can trolololo and the owner will never suspect you. Hours of enjoyment guaranteed!

*In the end of the process, after switching from a rare smiley to a hidden smiley, remember to switch back to your favorite smiley that resembles the troll inside you. Say something in the chat, like "Do not mess with TI²", "They see me trollin', they hatin'", "Welcome to a world full of troll. TI²" or "Another one bites the dust". Then, take a picture and upload it to this thread with the link and code to the level so others can enjoy it too!
* If you want to write TI² in the EE chat but you don't know how to make the squared sign, you need to hold Alt (or Func+Alt for laptops without numpad) and type 253 in the numpad. So that'd be Alt+253.
