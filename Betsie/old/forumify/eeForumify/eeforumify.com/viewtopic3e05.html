Five Different Kinds of Trouble

Starts with several illusions. Then requires lots and lots of very precise jumping.
Difficulty: medium for pro players, hard for regular players, unfinishable for casual players.
It'll take quite some time to reach the finish line, so a high level of stamina is required.
Good luck and have fun!
