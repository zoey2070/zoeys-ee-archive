The Supreme List of Rules
Respect the forum and we will respect you.

Be Nice  The Everybody Edits community is one composed of a variety of cultures, ages, languages, thoughts, and ideas. Please be respectful of your fellow players and dont forget that there is a human on the other end of the keyboard.
Dont Spam  No one likes to read nonsense or repeated posts, please make sure to think before you press the Submit button.
Safety/Security  EE hosts a wide variety of ages, from under 10 to over 50. We at EE hope to protect our members by reminding players to be aware that information posted on these forums is public, and we should never post any personal information about ourselves that could give someone an opportunity to pose a threat.
'Guardians' are not allowed to post here. Any evasions will result in an IP-ban.

Infraction Points* Expires
Off-topic post 1 2 months
Not understandable post 1 1 week

Do not...
• 2a: post useless, irrelevant, inappropriate, or incomprehensible content.
• 2b: post text with unreasonable effects.
• 2c: speak in a language the opening post is not written in.
• 2d: insult, annoy, or pester.
• 2e: give out personal information or information of others.
• 2f: use a proxy or try to evade a ban.
• 2g: provide illegal tools or directions (such as a link to a pirated game).
• 2h: use the report button to request stickies.
• 2i: make another account without permission.
• 2j: use more than four lines and/or more than 300px vertically in a signature: Example 300 max height signature

Often misunderstood rules -- You may...
• 3a: post multiple times in a row if they are about different, relevant, topics.
• 3b: use the report button to request a topic title change or a lock of your own topic.
• 3c: use negative criticism constructively.
• 3d: bump a thread if it has dropped past the first page. ("gravedig")
• 3e: make an alternate account with a staff member's permission.
