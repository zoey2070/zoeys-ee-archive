The Many Appearances of "S17"

The Many Appearances of "S17" is a new project, a level series, that I am going to start. It's about time travelling to different ages, but there's something very odd going on...




Industrial Grey


This is the first level in my series, The Many Appearances of "S17". The level is based off the Industrial Age Britain, and the factories and stuff that were put off. The art depicts a train going across a bridge over water, with factories in the background and a massive smoke cloud rising from all of them. Once you're in the smoke, it gets harder to see (literally, via 'eyerape'). You must find your way out of the cloud and into the light. Thanks to Jr2 for making some minis.

Play Industrial Grey



Mars City


I kept the art pretty simple in this level. I sticked to my ordinary style of shading, but I liked the look of the two-colour mountains, so I left that how it was. The water was tough to make, since I didn't make a proper sky, just space (I think in real life Mars might have a sky). I tried to make it a dark red; trying to make it look slightly transparent - the black of space with a bit of red from the rocks. The minis are a little bit lazy/rushed in places but I'm content with them. The art shows us a dome on Mars with a city inside, and the environment of Mars surrounding it. However, there is an unusual, seemingly unnecessary service for this city. An airport. What's more, the runway is marked with the familiar mark,"S17".

Play Mars City



Montezuma II



Here you are, once again at a random time in history. Somehow, this time the journey felt longer. This level was started a really long time ago, and I wasn't as good at designing levels back then as I am now. So that's why this level kinda sucks a bit. This level is slightly different to the others in the series. In the other levels, the path that the player takes is the actual path that the character in the story takes. In this level the path the player takes is more like what the character observes. A bit more of the story has been revealed in this level, so see if you can figure out the story behind S17.


Play Montezuma II
