Everybody Edits Event 2010/11: Winners and results!

The time has come to announce the winners of our very first level design contest!

1st prize winner: Who the F is Starblinky - The Clock Strikes 12
2nd prize winner: LX Crew - New year, New concept
3rd prize winner: Embur - Reversible New Year

It is finally time to announce the winners of the first Everybody Edits design contest. This has been really exciting for us to watch and moderate as we've seen you guys create som epic levels for everyone to play. However, only 3 of these maps will be rewarded. Each of the winners will be announced in separate posts in order for each of them to get some time in the spotlight.

Once the 3 winners have been announced, we will also post the full results for everyone to discuss.

Stay tuned, the 3rd prize winner is coming up real shortly!
