Rats in the Wall - Adventure map

Hello there,

I completly abandoned my intended map design with hot grills and started something I always wanted to do.
An epic adventure inspired by several Metroidvania games.

Rats in the Wall is a project name only and does not reflect the final state of the map.
So far I have a few rooms ready to play which involve easy minigames, jumping passages, some spikes and firepits.
Also a few switches and coins to collect.
I intend to make the gamer backtrack quite alot, forcing _you_ to focus on exploring instead of beating ultra hard minigames which I dislike - and these kind of minis do not fit onto this map.

Important: There are hidden passages and they are required - but they can be seen if you open your eyes a bit.
