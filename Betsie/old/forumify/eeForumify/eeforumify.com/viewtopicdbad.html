Re: 3-in-1 coindoor game

Thanks for the comments/reviews
My main goal was to make a world with 3 difficulty levels that didn't merge and where you would collect all coins on all levels. Due this I had to put in multiple switches (not the purple ones, but for smileys), costing me some room for minis. Another goal was to reinvent my coindoor levels (look at 'ONLY coindoors 496', a 25x25 (23x23) world with 496 coindoors and 30 coins) with coindoors and coingates (and probably some other gates/doors), which I tried in the top-right room and in the second room from left.
Those goals worked out really well (second one a bit less) and if anyone wants a challenge: do the same thing with better mini's (or better in another way). I can't promise that I'll look on the forums every week but I might find it in the lobby.
Thanks to everyone who played and gave a woot. 600 plays on the first day is a new record for me.
