Welcome to Block Bot, a simplistic bot to show who has placed a block and where in your world.

~How to Use Block Bot:~

Logging in:

Enter the world ID to the desired level that you want the bot to enter in the "World ID:" field. You can find the world ID by clicking the "Share Level" button on the bottom left of the screen when you're in the level. After "/games/" you should see a "PW" or a "BW" followed by a bunch of random characters. Copy it, starting with the "PW"/"BW" and paste it into the "World ID:" field in the bot.

Then, press the button with two plugs on it labelled "Connect" and the bot should enter the desired level as a guest.

Note: To connect to the level, there must be a player already in the world.

Disconnecting:

Simply press the button with two plugs on it labelled "Disconnect" button to disconnect from EE.

Using the bot:

Once the bot has connected, any time a player places a block in the level, it will be recorded in the "Blocks Placed" List Box.
